# DigitalSafeTwin_CMPG323

3 - Campus Safety Digital Twin	
Precious Masetla	
CampusSafeTwin is a campus safety digital twin prototype that gives students and campus protection services a live, map based view of safety conditions across a university campus. Students can report incidents such as theft or medical emergencies directly onto an interactive map while security staff monitor a real time dashboard showing incident locations, statuses and hotspot analytics. The system also recommends safer walking routes on campus based on historical incident patterns.
